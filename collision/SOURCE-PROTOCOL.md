# Magnetic source test

Use the BH-SCAT-001 close-limit geometry with half-separation 0.03M, Z=2.5, bare mass 0.20/M, c=1e−5 in code units and M=1. Initialize the scalar and its velocity to zero. Compare signed test-field amplitudes b=0,+1,−1,+2 at dx=0.2,0.1,0.05M, dt=dx/4, t≤36M. Use the same boundary conditions and detector at x=r*=20M. Preserve the separate incoming pulse and spherical/failed geometry records from BH-SCAT-001.

Prescribe A_phi = b s(r)(1−u²), u=cos(theta), s=r² exp[−(r−9)²/18]/2 with no time component and zero coordinate-time derivative. External currents maintaining this localized test field are prescribed implicitly by Maxwell's equation. Ignore its stress feedback on the metric and changes in the test field due to the scalar. Its invariant is computed through first order in the binary distortion; evolve zeroth-order scalar l=0,2 and the induced first-order l=0,2,4. Coordinates and positive/negative scalar values follow the archived C1 convention. This is a source-response initial-value test released from zero in an already magnetized environment. It does not establish how an accretion flow formed that field or a premerger equilibrium.

Before execution, require the zero arm to stay zero, identical traces under field reversal, and fourfold signed traces on doubling the field within relative L2 tolerance 1e−9 for each nonzero mode. Require the positive-source signal to be resolved against the last refinement change by a factor of ten for each mode at x=20M and require decreasing refinement differences. Compare the pulse extraction at dx=0.05 with its archived three modes (zero and ± seed, l=0,2). Display coordinate fields on finite radial shells. A source-dependent response is shared with an ordinary scalar–photon theory at matched normalization. The SXS orbital record is a separate GR calculation with its own origin of time.
